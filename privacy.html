Privacy Policy

cortes-gta6 is a private tool used only by the owner of this YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel.

Data accessed

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set thumbnails and schedule publication on the owner's own channel.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
